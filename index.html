Questions

BEFORE YOU CALL.

Do you come to me?
Yes. We're fully mobile across Mississauga and the GTA — driveway, condo garage, or office parking lot. Tell us where the car will be when you call.
Do you need my water or power?
No. We bring our own water and power, so we can work anywhere the car is parked.
How long does it take?
Silver runs about an hour and a half. Gold is three to four hours. Platinum is a full day. Condition and vehicle size can move those numbers.
What does it cost?
Price depends on the vehicle, the package, and how much work the car actually needs. Call and describe the car — we'll quote it on the spot.
How do I pay?
Cash, e-transfer, or card on completion.
How far ahead should I book?
Weekends fill first. Two or three days ahead is usually enough, and same-day is often possible mid-week.
